PROCEDURE

Prepare your backup in four steps.

01

Prepare

Have your Seed Phrase ready, then open the SATONA conversion tool available here. Each word will automatically be converted into its binary equivalent before marking.

02

Assemble

Unscrew the conical tip of the automatic center punch. Carefully remove the internal parts without mixing them up, then insert one of the two supplied tips with the point facing outward before screwing everything back together.

03

Mark

Place the tip in the center of the square to be marked and press until it clicks. Repeat the operation up to 8 times on the same square to create a deeper mark for maximum long-term durability.

04

Hide

Store your plate somewhere discreet, known only to you. Inside a wall, beneath a floorboard, in a safe, inside a hollow book or any hiding place worthy of protecting your treasure.

BIP39

BIP39, or Bitcoin Improvement Proposal 39, is a standard used by cryptocurrency wallets to generate recovery phrases made up of 12 to 24 words. These words are converted into a binary seed used to create the wallet’s cryptographic keys. Their index and binary representation are public, universal and documented, ensuring a long-lasting method that remains independent from SATONA.

11-bit

Each bit is either a 0 or a 1. With 11 bits, it is possible to represent 2,048 combinations, matching exactly the 2,048 words in the official BIP39 word list.

SATONA plates feature 24 rows of 11 squares, with 12 rows on the front and 12 on the back. This allows a complete 24-word Seed Phrase to be encoded using the automatic center punch included in the kit.
