Purchase FAQ

Everything to know before buying

What exactly does the SATONA kit contain?

One solid brass SATONA plate, an automatic punch, two tips, a premium magnetic closure box, a user guide and a QR Code providing access to the instructions.

How many words can I back up?

SATONA encodes up to 24 BIP39 words: words 1 to 12 on the front and words 13 to 24 on the back.

Is SATONA an electronic wallet?

No. SATONA is a physical backup medium. It does not store any digital assets.

Do I need to engrave the words themselves?

No. SATONA uses each word’s position in the BIP39 list to represent it in 11-bit binary form.

Is SATONA compatible with my wallet?

SATONA is compatible with recovery phrases that use the BIP39 standard.

Can I use SATONA without an Internet connection?

Yes. For a real recovery phrase, the recommended method is to download the BIP39 list and perform the conversion entirely offline.

Why use a metal plate?

A metal plate physically preserves the information needed to reconstruct your recovery phrase on a durable medium.

Should I enter my complete recovery phrase online?

No. Never enter your complete recovery phrase on a website. For real use, download the BIP39 list and work entirely offline.

Are my digital assets stored on the SATONA plate?

No. SATONA stores no digital assets or private key; it preserves only the information needed to reconstruct your recovery phrase.
