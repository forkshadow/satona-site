How to use your SATONA kit

4 steps to convert, mark, verify and protect your recovery phrase.

IMPORTANT

Never enter your complete recovery phrase online.

For real-world use, download the BIP39 list and work offline.

1 BIP39 word→11 bits→1 line

Each word corresponds to 11 positions.

Example:ability → 00000000001

01

Convert

Find the 11-bit binary equivalent of each word in your recovery phrase.

Always preserve the exact order of the words.

For real-world use, prefer the downloaded BIP39 list and work offline.

02

Assemble the center punch

Unscrew the conical end of the automatic center punch, insert one of the two supplied tips, then screw it back together.

The center punch is now ready to use.

No hammer or electrical power is required.

03

Mark

Work line by line, in the exact order of your recovery phrase.

  • position the tip in the center of the square;
  • press vertically until it clicks;
  • repeat the impact on the same square up to 8 times, until the mark is clear and durable.

For each 0 bit, leave the square empty.

1 = MARK0 = LEAVE EMPTY

04

Verify before storage

Before hiding your plate, carefully verify each line.

Check the word position, its binary code and the squares actually marked.

A marking error can prevent your wallet from being restored correctly.
