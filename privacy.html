The French version is the reference for seller legal information.

SATONA is the controller. Its full legal identity and address must be completed before publication. Contact: contact@satona.fr.

Order data is used to perform the contract and meet legal obligations. Support email is processed to answer requests and pursue legitimate customer-service interests. Analytics is based on consent. Technical logs may be processed for legitimate security interests.

Recipients and processors

Data may be received by SATONA and, where used, Stripe for payment/checkout, Google Analytics after consent, and the hosting provider. The hosting provider’s identity and any operational delivery providers must be completed before publication.

Transfers outside the EEA

Stripe or Google may involve transfers outside the EEA under their applicable safeguards. Their current documentation should be reviewed before publication.

Retention

No verified retention schedule is available in the repository. Contract, accounting, support, analytics and log retention periods must be documented before publication and limited to what is necessary.

Your rights

Cookies

Necessary storage supports consent choices. Google Analytics is blocked by default and loads only after acceptance. Use Cookie preferences in the footer to accept, refuse or change the choice at any time.

Contact

Exercise your rights at contact@satona.fr. Identity evidence may be requested only when necessary.
