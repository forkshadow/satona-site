Learn the role of wallets, exchanges, self-custody and hardware wallets.

FROM SEED TO WALLET

A seed phrase derives private keys, which control addresses used by a wallet. The wallet coordinates access; it does not hold coins inside the device.

SEED PHRASEPRIVATE KEYSADDRESSESWALLET

SATONA Academy

SIGN WITHOUT EXPOSING THE KEY

A transaction enters the hardware wallet, is checked and signed, then returns to the network. The private key remains isolated inside the device.

TRANSACTIONHARDWARE WALLETSIGNATURENETWORK

SATONA Academy

TWO CUSTODY MODELS

With an exchange, a third party controls the keys and infrastructure. With self-custody, you control the keys and accept the security duties. Neither model removes risk; responsibilities differ.
